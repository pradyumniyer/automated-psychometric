# Pain-point backlog: ranked by value vs effort (cofounder view)

**Judgement, not market research.** Validate the top items with 5–10 real users before building billing around them.
Positioning: *get raw survey data analysis-ready in minutes, and prove what you did.*

| # | Pain point | Feature | Value | Effort | Phase |
|---|-----------|---------|-------|--------|-------|
| 1 | "What did I exclude and why?" for thesis committees and reviewers | **Cleaning log + methods paragraph export** (we already track exclusions and reasons) | High | Low | 1 stretch / 3 |
| 2 | Variable names and codes nobody remembers | **Codebook / data dictionary export** | High | Low | 1 stretch / 3 |
| 3 | Survey-tool exports with extra header rows | **Import presets** (Qualtrics, Google Forms) on top of the existing header-row and question-label controls | High | Low | 1 stretch / 2 |
| 4 | Scoring right/wrong tests from answer keys | **Answer-key scoring + item analysis** | High | Medium | 2 |
| 5 | Choosing which items to keep from an expert panel | **Content validity** | High (scale developers) | Medium | 2 |
| 6 | Merging pre/post data; IDs that don't match | **Longitudinal with ID-match suggestions** | High | Medium | 4 |
| 7 | Getting SPSS/R to show labels again after cleaning | **Analysis-ready export pack** (CSV/XLSX + label syntax) | Medium–High | Medium | 3 |
| 8 | Careless responders beyond straight-lining | **Duplicates, speeders, attention-check flags** | Medium–High | Low | 2 (UI approval) |
| 9 | Beep-level files too messy for multilevel models | **EMA helpers: compliance, centering, lags** | Medium (niche) | Medium–High | 5 |
| 10 | Wide ↔ long reshaping outside longitudinal | Generic reshape tool | Medium | Medium | 6 |
| 11 | Coding open-ended text | Qualitative tools | Unknown | High | Later |

## Notes
- Items 1–3 are the cheapest way to make the free tier feel like a complete product. Consider them part of the launch promise.
- Items 4, 5 and 8 are good candidates for the first Pro features (they serve scale developers and test analysts, who tend to have stronger need and budget). Test this assumption with users.
- Do not build item 9 before there is demand evidence; EMA researchers are a narrower audience.
